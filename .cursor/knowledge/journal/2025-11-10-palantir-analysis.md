### PEG Ratio Analysis
**PEG = P/E Ratio / Earnings Growth Rate**

- **P/E**: 246 (forward)
- **Earnings Growth**: 200%
- **PEG**: 246 / 200 = **1.23**

**Context:**
- PEG < 1: Potentially undervalued
- PEG 1-2: Reasonable for high growth
- PEG > 2: Potentially overvalued

**Insight**: Palantir's PEG of 1.23 suggests the valuation may be more reasonable when adjusted for growth, but it's still high.

### Is 246 P/E Too High for Software Sector?

**Answer: Yes, it's very high, but context matters:**

1. **Compared to mature software**: Much higher (MSFT 35, ADBE 20)
2. **Compared to high-growth software**: Higher than profitable peers (NOW 104)
3. **Many high-growth peers**: Are unprofitable (no P/E)
4. **Palantir's advantages**: Profitable with very high growth (62.8%)
5. **PEG context**: 1.23 is more reasonable when adjusted for growth

**Risk**: Any growth slowdown could compress P/E significantly.
