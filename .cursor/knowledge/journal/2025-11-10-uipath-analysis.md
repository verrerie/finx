## Business Model

**What They Do:**
UiPath provides an end-to-end automation platform offering robotic process automation (RPA) solutions. Founded in 2005, headquartered in New York, New York.

**Products:**
- **RPA Platform**: Software robots that emulate human actions to automate repetitive tasks
- **AI/ML/NLP Capabilities**: Embedded AI, machine learning, and natural language processing for decision-making
- **Automation Cloud**: Cloud-based automation platform
- **Process Mining**: Analyzes digital footprint of users' processes across systems
- **Agent Builder**: Agentic automation solutions (recent product)

**Market:**
- Robotic Process Automation (RPA) market
- Enterprise automation solutions
- Financial services, healthcare, manufacturing, retail, public sector

**Recent Strategic Developments:**
- **Product Innovation**: Agent Builder, Agentic Orchestration, Agentic Testing
- **Acquisitions**: Acquired Peak AI for vertical-specific agents (pricing, inventory management)
- **Cloud Adoption**: Cloud ARR grew over 50% year-over-year
